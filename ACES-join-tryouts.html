1

10 & Under

Email Barbie Barker at Barbie1@acesswimclub.com to schedule a drop-in tryout or the next Rookie Camp. Need a 25 (8&U) or 50 (9–10) of each stroke without stopping.

Colorado context

Built for this altitude, this calendar

Short course, then long course

Like the rest of Colorado Swimming, ACES trains yards from September into March (state, Far Westerns, Sectionals) and meters from spring through late July (Age Group LC State, Senior Zones, Futures, Juniors). New families do not have to know that vocabulary on day one — coaches place you and explain the meet path.

Two sides of town, one club

  • West: Heritage HS, Arapahoe HS, Goodson Rec, Schlessman YMCA
  • East: Smoky Hill HS, Cherokee Trail HS, Aurora YMCA
  • Summer long course: Lowry Pool, Denver

Group assignment follows the swimmer, then we match the closest honest practice site.

What tryout day looks like

Bring a suit. Leave with a group recommendation.

In the water

  • Coaches watch stroke legality, endurance for the age, and how your swimmer takes a cue.
  • This is not a one-heat “make team / don’t make team” show. Some athletes need a second look or a Rookie Camp week.
  • Not every swimmer is placed. Better an honest no than a group that will crush a kid.

What to pack

  • Competitive suit, cap, goggles, towel, water bottle
  • Any recent times (summer league or USA Swimming) — helpful, not required
  • A parent on deck for pickup and the 10-minute placement conversation

Fall short-course practices typically start the week after Labor Day. Mid-season joins are normal if a coach has seen the swimmer.

Parents ask first

Straight answers

Can a summer-league only swimmer try out?

Yes. Queens and the 10 & Under groups are built for that jump. We still need legal strokes at the distances listed above.

We play another sport. Is that allowed?

Age-group coaches expect you to say so up front. Queens in particular welcomes multi-sport athletes. Senior Elite is a different contract — year-round swimming is the job.

Do we have to swim every meet?

Most groups plan on about one meet a month. Kings and senior groups also expect team-hosted meets and championships. Coaches will not surprise you with a hidden travel circuit on week one.

Where do I send a transfer from another USA team?

Same tryout path, plus your current club’s blessing to unattach / transfer. High schoolers start with Tom.
