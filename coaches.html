Head of the program

Tom Byorick

Head coach

Notre Dame swimmer and Heritage High School head coach. Joined ACES in the mid-1990s after summer-league and Smoky Hill work; built the senior sprint group that sent packs of athletes to Junior Nationals, then shifted the club toward versatile, all-event swimming. Still the person to call for high-school and Senior placement.

tom1@acesswimclub.com

Barbie Barker

Associate head · operations

Joined ACES in 2009 from Michigan. Director of Operations, Jokers and Senior Prep coach, Arapahoe girls and Heritage boys assistant, Colorado Swimming Administrative Vice Chair, ASCA member. First inbox for 10 & Under tryouts and Rookie Camp.

barbie1@acesswimclub.com
