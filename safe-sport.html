Who gets the report

Two doors, on purpose

U.S. Center for SafeSport

Exclusive jurisdiction for sexual misconduct, sexual harassment, sexually explicit or inappropriate communication, child sexual abuse, and related criminal dispositions. You may also report physical or emotional misconduct and MAAPP issues there.

uscenterforsafesport.org

USA Swimming Safe Sport

Code of Conduct issues, many physical or emotional misconduct reports, MAAPP violations, and other USA Swimming policy matters that are not in the Center’s exclusive lane. Use the national form or the anonymous text line 888-270-SWIM (7946).

usaswimming.org/safe-sport/report-a-concern

Adults authorized to interact with minor athletes who learn facts that give reason to suspect child abuse, including sexual or physical abuse, must report to law enforcement and/or Colorado child protection and the U.S. Center for SafeSport within 24 hours. That is federal law and USA Swimming’s mandatory reporting rule — not an optional club preference.

What ACES will and will not do

Do not only tell a coach

Reporting

Telling someone on deck is not the same as a report to the Center or USA Swimming. Use the official forms. You may copy the head coach or operations director after you have reported, not instead of reporting.

MAAPP on our decks

Prevention

USA Swimming’s Minor Athlete Abuse Prevention Policy covers one-on-one interactions, travel, locker rooms, electronic communication, and similar limits. Coaches and meet staff follow that policy at Heritage, Arapahoe, Cherokee Trail, Lowry, and every other ACES site.

Coaches complete required Safe Sport training. Parents and athletes should take the USA Swimming education modules for their role. Ask the office if your account does not show the current course.

Short answers

Parents ask

Can I report anonymously?

USA Swimming accepts anonymous tips at 888-270-7946. The Center’s portal also allows reports without identifying yourself. Anonymous reports can limit follow-up. If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 and use your name with law enforcement.

What if the concern is “just” a code-of-conduct or bullying issue?

Still use USA Swimming’s report-a-concern path. Club hallway conversations do not create a record. Physical and emotional misconduct can sit with USA Swimming or move to the Center after intake.

Who at ACES can answer process questions?

Start with Safe Sport Club Coordinator Linda Eaton. For LSC-level questions, contact Colorado Swimming Safe Sport Chair Suzanne Tennyson. Head Coach Tom Byorick (tom1@acesswimclub.com) and Associate Head Coach / Operations Barbie Barker (barbie1@acesswimclub.com) can point you to policy and training. None of these people replace the Center, USA Swimming, or law enforcement.
