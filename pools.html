Pool locations

South metro water, not one building.

ACES does not own a single home pool. Short course lives on school and rec decks from Littleton to Aurora. Long course in summer is at Lowry. The office on South Hudson Circle is not a practice site. Your coach names the pool for the week — this page is how you find the door.

Before you drive

Three rules

1

Placement before parking

A tryout assigns the group. The group assigns the site. Do not pick a pool because it is closer to home.

2

School decks change

High school pools follow school calendars, sports banquets, and lockouts. Cancellations belong on Home / News, not in a text thread only.

3

Watch the bumps

Heritage’s parking-lot speed bumps are as advertised. Arrive once in daylight if you have never been.

West side

Littleton · Centennial · south Denver

Heritage High School

West · short course

1401 W Geddes Ave, Littleton, CO 80120. Neighborhood near S. Broadway and W. Mineral. On W. Geddes, east of S. Windermere, turn into the lot. Pool is at the bottom of the lot — glass wall faces parking. Speed bumps are sharp.

Arapahoe High School

West · short course

2201 E Dry Creek Rd, Littleton, CO 80122. S. University and E. Dry Creek. Be on Dry Creek west of University, turn north on S. Franklin, first right into the lot. Pool is in the corner of the school. Used for West-side age-group work and some tryouts.

Goodson Rec Center

6315 S University Blvd, Centennial, CO 80121. City rec facility — follow posted public hours and any ACES lane signs on the deck.

Schlessman YMCA

West · Y deck

3901 E Yale Ave, Denver, CO 80210. West-side Y site. Check in at the Y desk if the building requires member or guest passage after hours.

East side

Aurora · east Centennial

Cherokee Trail High School

East · short course

25901 E Arapahoe Pkwy N, Aurora, CO 80016. Off E. Smoky Hill and E. Arapahoe. On Arapahoe east of Smoky Hill, first left down the long drive along the school. Pool is at the far end next to the tennis courts.

Map

Smoky Hill High School

East · short course

Used for East-side groups and 11–14 drop-in tryouts (evenings in the spring tryout flyer). Confirm the exact door with the East-side coach the first week — school campuses have more than one entrance.

Map

Aurora / Wheatlands YMCA

East · Y deck

East-side Y water. 10 & Under drop-in tryouts have used the Aurora Y on Monday/Wednesday evenings. Treat the Y front desk rules as part of practice: late pickup still has to exit through their building.

Meters + camps

Long course and first-look water

Lowry Pool

Summer long course

775 Akron Way, Denver, CO 80230. Old Lowry Air Force Base, now a neighborhood. From E. 6th and Havana: west on Havana to the roundabout, right on Yosemite, first right on Severn (it jogs), first left on Akron. Pool on the left. Park along Akron or just north of the pool.

Map

Homestead Pool

Rookie camp · 10&U

6767 E Easter Ave, Centennial, CO 80112. North Pool at The Homestead in the Willows — used for 10 & Under Rookie / tryout camp, not as a default year-round site.

Map
