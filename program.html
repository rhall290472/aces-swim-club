Program information · 2026–27

Seven groups. One year-round season.

Short course (yards) runs September into March. Long course (meters) runs spring through late July. A coach places the group after a tryout. Multi-sport athletes belong in Queens and the 10 & Under groups if the strokes are legal — Senior Elite is a different contract.

The deck

Groups

Jokers

8 & under · $135 / mo

First year-round home after lessons or summer league. Swim a 25 of each stroke without stopping. Practice 2–3 times a week. About one meet a month. West-side lead: Barbie Barker.

Jacks

10 & under · $145 / mo

Four 50s without stopping. Technique first, then race skills. West Jacks: Tim Byers and Kim Garcia. East Jacks follow the East-side age-group staff.

Queens

11–14 · $160 / mo

About 90 minutes, 3+ practices a week, one meet a month. Working toward Silver State times. Multi-sport and summer-league graduates are welcome if 100s of all four strokes are legal. West Queens: Andrea Sheremeta.

Kings

11–14 · $170 / mo

Silver State depth (the published bar has been seven 11–12 Silver State times for Kings). Practices offered six days; five encouraged. Team-hosted and championship meets expected. West Kings: Brett Kelley.

Senior Prep

Bridge · $170 / mo

The week starts to look like high school swimming before the varsity calendar does. Barbie Barker is listed as Senior Prep coach. High-school-aged new members still start with Tom.

Senior

High school · $200 / mo

Fourteen and older, all four strokes, year-round short and long course. Goal-oriented attendance toward varsity, state, and Sectionals. Contact: Tom Byorick.

Senior Elite

National track · $215 / mo

Highest load. Sectional / Futures / Junior National path. 90% attendance is the standard. Year-round swimming is the job — say so before you try out for this group.
